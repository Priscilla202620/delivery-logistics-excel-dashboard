# Delivery Logistics Performance Dashboard -- Excel

## Project Overview

This project analyses **25,000 delivery records** to evaluate logistics
performance and identify factors contributing to delivery delays.

Using **Microsoft Excel**, the dataset was cleaned, validated and
analysed using formulas, PivotTables and PivotCharts. The results were
presented in a dashboard containing key performance indicators (KPIs),
charts and business insights.

The analysis focuses on delivery performance across **delivery partners,
delivery modes, weather conditions, regions, package types and vehicle
types**.

## Business Objective

The objective of this project was to understand overall delivery
performance and identify the main factors associated with delivery
delays.

### Key Questions

-   What percentage of deliveries are completed on time?
-   Which delivery modes experience the highest delays?
-   How do weather conditions affect delivery performance?
-   Which delivery partners have the highest and lowest delay rates?
-   Do region, package type or vehicle type have a significant impact on
    delays?
-   What is the average delivery time, delivery cost and customer
    rating?

## Data Cleaning and Preparation

Before performing the analysis, the dataset was reviewed and cleaned to
ensure that the information could be analysed correctly.

The following steps were performed:

-   Checked the dataset for blank values.
-   Checked for duplicate records.
-   Reviewed the data types of important columns.
-   Cleaned the Delivery Time and Expected Delivery Time fields.
-   Converted delivery cost values into a usable numeric format.
-   Created clean delivery-time columns for analysis.
-   Created a Delivery Performance field to classify deliveries.
-   Validated delivery performance against the original delayed
    indicator.
-   Checked the cleaned data before creating PivotTables and KPIs.

## Analysis Performed

### Delivery Mode Analysis

Delivery performance was compared across the different delivery modes.

**Express deliveries had a 73.78% delay rate**, making Express delivery
the strongest area of concern.

Standard delivery performed considerably better, with the dataset
showing no delayed deliveries in this category.

### Weather Condition Analysis

Delivery performance was analysed across different weather conditions.

The highest delay rates were:

-   **Stormy weather -- 41.45%**
-   **Rainy weather -- 37.35%**
-   **Foggy weather -- 30.32%**

Clear, cold and hot weather conditions recorded considerably lower delay
rates.

This suggests that difficult weather conditions may have an important
impact on delivery performance.

### Delivery Partner Analysis

Delivery partners were compared using their percentage of delayed
deliveries.

-   **Xpressbees:** highest delay rate at **28.27%**
-   **Delhivery:** lowest delay rate at **24.80%**

Although differences between delivery partners were smaller than those
found between delivery modes and weather conditions, the analysis still
identified opportunities for comparing partner performance.

### Region Analysis

Delivery performance was analysed across Central, East, North, South and
West regions.

The delay rates across the regions were relatively similar, suggesting
that **region was not one of the strongest factors associated with
delivery delays in this dataset**.

### Package Type Analysis

Different package types, including electronics, groceries, documents,
furniture, pharmacy products and fragile items, were analysed.

Delay rates across package types were relatively close to one another.
This suggests that **package type did not show a major difference in
delivery performance** compared with factors such as delivery mode and
weather.

### Vehicle Type Analysis

Delivery performance was compared across bikes, EV bikes, EV vans,
scooters, trucks and vans.

The delay percentages were very similar across vehicle types. Therefore,
**vehicle type did not appear to be a major driver of delivery delays
within this dataset**.

## Key Insights

1.  **Express delivery is the biggest performance concern.** Express
    deliveries recorded a **73.78% delay rate**.
2.  **Poor weather conditions are associated with higher delays.**
    Stormy weather recorded a **41.45% delay rate**, followed by rainy
    weather at **37.35%**.
3.  **Delivery partner performance varies.** Xpressbees recorded the
    highest partner delay rate at **28.27%**, while Delhivery recorded
    the lowest at **24.80%**.
4.  **Overall delivery performance is relatively strong.** **73.32%** of
    deliveries were on time, while **26.68%** were delayed.

## Business Recommendations

-   Investigate the operational process used for **Express deliveries**
    to understand why the delay rate is significantly higher.
-   Consider additional delivery planning and contingency measures
    during **stormy and rainy weather conditions**.
-   Review delivery-partner performance and investigate the processes
    used by better-performing partners.
-   Monitor delivery KPIs regularly to identify changes in delay rates.
-   Focus improvement efforts primarily on **delivery mode and
    weather-related risks**, as region, package type and vehicle type
    showed relatively small differences.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Excel can be used to transform a large
logistics dataset into meaningful business information.

The analysis identified **delivery mode and weather conditions as the
strongest areas associated with delivery delays**, while differences
across regions, package types and vehicle types were relatively small.

The final dashboard provides a simple way for stakeholders to monitor
delivery performance and identify areas that may require further
investigation or operational improvement.
